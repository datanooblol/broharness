# Knock-knock jokes

- Knock knock. Who's there? Lettuce. Lettuce who? Lettuce in, it's cold out here!
- Knock knock. Who's there? Boo. Boo who? Aw, don't cry, it's just a joke.
- Knock knock. Who's there? Interrupting cow. Interrupting cow w— MOO!
- Knock knock. Who's there? Woo. Woo who? Glad you're excited too!
- Knock knock. Who's there? Cash. Cash who? No thanks, I prefer peanuts.
