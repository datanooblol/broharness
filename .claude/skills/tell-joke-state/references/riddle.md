# Riddle jokes

A riddle joke poses a question and lets the listener guess before revealing an
answer that's clever, absurd, or punny rather than literally correct.

## Characteristics

- Question-then-answer structure: "Why did/does/is [X]?" followed by a pause,
  then the answer.
- The answer should surprise -- either a pun, an absurd literal twist, or an
  unexpected logical leap, not the real-world answer to the question.
- Works well framed as genuine trivia at first ("Why did the chicken cross the
  road?") so the punchline's silliness lands as a contrast.
- Keep the question itself short and plausible-sounding -- the answer carries
  the joke, not the setup.

Use this as a style guide to write a fresh, original riddle joke on the
requested topic -- not a script to copy from verbatim.
